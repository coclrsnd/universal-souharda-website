About the Society

Built on trust. Driven by service. Growing together.

Universal Souharda Co-op Society Ltd. was established in 2023 with a clear purpose: to create a trusted, member-focused financial institution that combines cooperative strength with the possibilities of modern technology.

From the beginning, the society has focused on building meaningful relationships, encouraging savings, supporting genuine financial needs, and contributing to the economic development of the community.
