Deposit schemes

Member-friendly deposit options for saving, planning, and steady financial growth.

Universal Souharda offers practical deposit choices designed for families, traders, businesses, and members who want safe, transparent, and disciplined savings solutions.

Everyday accounts

Accounts designed for savings habits and regular transactions

01

Savings Bank Account

The Universal Souharda Savings Bank Account is designed to provide a safe, convenient, and flexible way to manage your everyday finances while building a strong savings habit.

Key Highlights

  • Member-focused savings account
  • Safe and convenient banking
  • Attractive interest on savings
  • Easy deposits and withdrawals
  • Nomination facility
  • Suitable for individuals and families
  • 24/7 access through available digital channels

Conditions

  • Minimum Balance - Rs. 500
  • Interest will be paid at the rate applicable to the Savings Bank account and as per the society's prevailing policy
02

Current Account

Designed for businesses, traders and institutions with regular banking transactions. The Universal Souharda Current Account is designed to provide convenient day-to-day banking for eligible members who require frequent deposits, withdrawals, collections, and business transactions.

Key Highlights

  • Designed for business transactions
  • Convenient day-to-day banking
  • Easy deposits and withdrawals
  • Suitable for traders, businesses and institutions
  • Cheque facility, where applicable
  • Efficient collection and payment services
  • Transparent service and charges
  • Member-focused financial service

Conditions

  • Minimum Quarterly Average Balance: 10000
  • Overdraft Facility: Available on collateral or turnover basis
Planned savings

Options for disciplined monthly saving and fixed-tenure growth

03

Recurring Deposit

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is a savings scheme where a member deposits a fixed amount every month for a selected period. At maturity, the member receives the accumulated deposits along with applicable interest.

Key Highlights

  • Regular monthly savings
  • Fixed monthly instalment
  • Disciplined savings habit
  • Attractive interest as per applicable rate
  • Flexible tenure options
  • Planned maturity benefit
  • Nomination facility
  • Premature closure subject to applicable rules
  • Suitable for individuals and families
  • Safe and transparent member service

Conditions & Features

  • Late Instalment: Charges may apply as per the approved schedule
  • RD Amount - minimum 500
  • Tenure - 12, 24, 36, or 60 months
  • Loan facility available against RD balance (as per society rules)
04

Fixed Deposit

Invest your savings with Universal Souharda Co-op Society Ltd. and earn attractive returns for a selected tenure. Our Fixed Deposit scheme is designed to help members achieve their financial goals with disciplined, secure, and planned savings.

Key Highlights

  • Nomination facility may be provided as per applicable rules
  • Attractive interest rates
  • Senior citizen 0.5% extra
  • Loan or overdraft facility available against the deposit (as per society rules)
  • Suitable for individuals and families
  • Safe and transparent service
  • Member-focused financial solution

Conditions

  • Minimum deposit: 1000
  • Tenure: 12, 24, 36, or 60 months
Special schemes

Flexible choices for monthly income and regular small savings

05

MIS (Monthly Installment Scheme)

Universal Souharda's Monthly Interest Scheme (MIS) is designed for members who want to invest a lump sum for a selected tenure and receive interest at regular monthly intervals, subject to the approved scheme terms.

06

Pigmy Deposit

Save Small. Save Regularly. Build a Better Future.

The Universal Souharda Pigmy Deposit is designed to encourage regular savings through small, frequent deposits. It is especially suitable for individuals, small businesses, traders, and members who prefer to save conveniently on a daily or regular basis.
