About us

Built on Trust. Driven by Service. Growing Together.

Our Story

Growing together through trust, service, and community purpose

Universal Souharda Co-op Society Ltd. was built with a clear purpose — to create a trusted, member-focused financial institution that combines the strength of cooperative values with the possibilities of modern technology.

Our journey began with a simple belief: financial growth becomes stronger when people grow together.

From the beginning, Universal Souharda has focused on building meaningful relationships with members, encouraging savings, supporting genuine financial needs, and contributing to the economic development of the community.

Our Journey

We are building Universal Souharda around three important foundations

Trust

Maintaining transparency, integrity, and confidence in every relationship.

Service

Understanding our members and providing simple, accessible, and responsive financial services.

Technology

Using modern digital solutions to make financial services more convenient, efficient, and secure.

"From a shared vision to a trusted financial future - growing together, serving together."

Our Mission

"To provide reliable, transparent, and accessible financial services that empower our members to save, grow, and achieve their financial goals, while embracing technology, maintaining strong cooperative values, and contributing to the sustainable development of our community."

"Trusted service. Responsible finance. Shared growth."

Our Vision

"To build a trusted, progressive and member-centric cooperative financial institution that empowers individuals, strengthens families, and contributes to the sustainable prosperity of our community."

"Building trust. Empowering members. Growing together."
